Cancellation and Refund Policy

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds are handled for the Christ Church Alumni Association digital membership platform.

Membership Activation

Alumni memberships are activated digitally after successful payment and confirmation.

Refund Policy

Membership payments are non-refundable once activated.

Cancellation Requests

If you believe a payment was made in error before activation, you may contact the alumni administration team for review. Any exception remains at the sole discretion of the platform administrators.
