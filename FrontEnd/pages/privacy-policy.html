Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Christ Church Alumni Association collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information shared through this digital alumni membership platform.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, graduation year, department, and membership-related details when you register or subscribe to the alumni platform.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is used to manage alumni registration, maintain membership records, communicate alumni updates, organize events, and improve the services provided through the platform.

3. Payment Information

Payments for alumni membership may be processed through a third-party payment gateway. We do not store full card or sensitive banking information on this website.

4. Data Sharing

Personal information is not sold to third parties. Data may be shared only with trusted service providers where required for payment processing, platform operations, or legal compliance.

5. Data Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect personal information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Contact

For privacy-related concerns, contact alumni@christchurchschool.org.
